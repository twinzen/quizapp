[MISTAKE]
WRONG: floated gentaly.
CORRECT: floated gently.
DESCRIPTION: The adverb 'gently' is misspelled here as 'gentaly'. Make sure to drop the 'a' when forming this adverb.
[/MISTAKE]

[MISTAKE]
WRONG: It shimmered and glow
CORRECT: It shimmered and glowed
DESCRIPTION: Keep your past-tense verbs consistent throughout the sentence; 'shimmered' should be matched with 'glowed'.
[/MISTAKE]

[MISTAKE]
WRONG: In side
CORRECT: Inside
DESCRIPTION: 'Inside' should be written as a single word rather than two separate words.
[/MISTAKE]

[MISTAKE]
WRONG: pale colour light
CORRECT: pale-coloured light
DESCRIPTION: Use the hyphenated adjective 'pale-coloured' to describe the noun 'light'.
[/MISTAKE]

[MISTAKE]
WRONG: star-shaped cusions
CORRECT: star-shaped cushions
DESCRIPTION: Remember to include the letter 'h' in 'cushions'.
[/MISTAKE]

[MISTAKE]
WRONG: When I walked in a gorgeous alien-fairy met me.
CORRECT: When I walked in, a gorgeous alien-fairy met me.
DESCRIPTION: Place a comma after an introductory clause like 'When I walked in' to give your reader a brief pause.
[/MISTAKE]
