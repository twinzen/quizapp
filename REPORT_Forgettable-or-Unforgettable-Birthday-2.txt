Forgetable or Unforgetable Birthday

Keroro will never forget his nineth birthday. He desperately wanted his birthday to be prefect. Weeks before the party, he asked his friend, Dororo, to use his gargantuan mansion because it was sparkly and grand.

On the day, Dororo decorated the ballroom with green balloons. Keroro joyously skipped to Dororo’s enormous house. The smell of fruit cake danced to the hallway. The music played lively. The cute small tables piled with cupcakes and macaroon and slushie. Keroro couldn’t stop smiling.

“What?” Keroro cried. “It is two o’clock already.” He rushed to the windows to find no one waiting outside. Suddenly, Dororo brought some invatations, to find it was Keroro’s!

“Keroro! Did you forget to send the cards?” said Dororo worriedly. The party was ruined, as no one was coming to the party. Keroro’s hands were shaking and his smile disappeared.

Luckily, the ninja, Dororo, could run and jump quickly to the rescue. Together, they gave the invatations to Sumomo, Tamama, Giroro and Kululu. All the friends dashed to Dororo’s mansion to join the party.

Finaly, the party started and everyone cheered loudly. They played games and danced until they got tired. It was the BEST day EVER! Looking back, Keroro realised he had to be less forgetful and realized what friends are for.

[REFERENCE_WRITING]
A Forgettable or Unforgettable Birthday?

Keroro would never forget his ninth birthday. Desperate for the celebration to be absolutely perfect, weeks before the event he had asked his friend, Dororo, if he could use his gargantuan mansion—a grand, sparkling place fit for a king.

When the big day finally arrived, Dororo decorated the magnificent ballroom with emerald-green balloons. Humming happily, Keroro skipped all the way to the grand house. As he stepped through the door, the mouth-watering aroma of freshly baked fruit cake drifted down the hallway while cheerful music echoed across the room. Small, neatly arranged tables groaned under the weight of delicious cupcakes, delicate macarons, and colourful slushies. Keroro was beaming with pride.

"What?" gasped Keroro, glancing at his watch. "It's two o'clock already!" He rushed to the tall glass windows, but no one was waiting outside. Suddenly, Dororo walked into the room carrying a mysterious stack of colourful envelopes. To Keroro's horror, they were his own party invitations!

"Keroro, did you forget to send these?" Dororo asked anxiously. In an instant, Keroro's trembling hands dropped to his sides as his smile completely disappeared—the party was completely ruined.

Fortunately, Dororo was an agile ninja who could leap into action at a moment's notice. Dodging past trees and vaulting over fences, he sprinted across town to the rescue. Together, the loyal friends hand-delivered the invitations to Sumomo, Tamama, Giroro, and Kululu. Without losing a second, all four friends dashed straight to the mansion to join the fun.

At long last, the party started, and everyone cheered at the top of their lungs. They played exciting games and danced until their legs were weak. It was truly the best day ever. Looking back, Keroro knew he had to be far less forgetful, but more importantly, he had discovered what true friends were really for.
[/REFERENCE_WRITING]
